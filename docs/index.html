SCARECROW FLASHER

ANUBIS DARKWATCH
RECEIVE-ONLY SENSOR FIRMWARE

Flash any device in the Scarecrow fleet straight from your browser — no IDE, no drivers. Plug the board in over USB, click Flash, and pick its serial port. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web Serial isn't in Safari/Firefox or on phones).

Evil Crow — CollectorESP32

The hub. Scans sub-GHz, serves the web UI + fox hunt, and collects direction-finding from rover nodes.

Open in Chrome or Edge to flash.

CYD HUD — 2.8" / 2.4"ESP32 · ILI9341

Handheld display for the collector: LOCATE, signatures, monitor, and band control. For ILI9341 Cheap Yellow Displays.

Open in Chrome or Edge to flash.

CYD HUD — 3.2"ESP32 · ST7789 · cap touch

Same HUD for the 3.2" IPS CYD (ESP32-2432S032) with GT911 capacitive touch.

Open in Chrome or Edge to flash.

T-Embed CC1101 — NodeESP32-S3

Headless direction-finding rover. Reports RSSI + burst fingerprints to the Evil Crow over ESP-NOW.

Open in Chrome or Edge to flash.

Cardputer ADV — NodeESP32-S3 · Cap CC1101

DF rover with its own screen + keyboard. Needs the M5Stack Cap CC1101 installed.

Open in Chrome or Edge to flash.
  1. Connect the board over USB.
  2. Click the Flash button for that device.
  3. Pick the serial port (hold BOOT/G0 while plugging in if it doesn't appear).
  4. Let it erase + install, then reconnect to the Scarecrow Wi-Fi.
Receive-only. No transmit path in any Scarecrow firmware.
